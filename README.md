# Levain & Loaf

A sourdough calculator that runs in the browser. It has no install, no account and no server, just one `index.html` file.

## What it does

- Works out grams for every ingredient from baker's percentages (hydration, levain, salt, whole grain), by total dough weight or total flour, for any number of loaves. It subtracts the flour and water already in the levain.
- Plans a levain build at any ratio (for example 1:2:2) and estimates when it will peak at your room temperature.
- Calculates the water temperature that hits your target dough temperature, for hand or stand-mixer mixing.
- Builds a timeline back from your mix time: levain feed, autolyse, folds, end of bulk, shaping, cold proof and bake.
- Gives suggestions that react to the formula, such as hydration versus whole-grain content, salt range, fermentation speed and the rise to aim for before shaping.
- Supports °F and °C and remembers your last settings in the browser.

Fermentation and peak times are estimates. Read the dough, not the clock.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repo.
